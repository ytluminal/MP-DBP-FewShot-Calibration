# Supplementary data

`MP-DBPs_SI.xlsx` is the unmodified workbook provided by the authors on 2026-11-11. It contains sheets `Ref 1-27`, `Ref 28-30`, and `Ref list`.

Do not reorder columns, impute missing values, or update descriptors when attempting to reproduce existing frozen model predictions. Historical model inputs under `workflows/` are preserved because their values may belong to earlier versions. The curators did not audit every source-paper experimental treatment against the manuscript inclusion rules in this packaging step.

`MP-DBPs_data_SM.xlsx` is the final supplementary workbook supplied on 26 November 2026. The historical workbook and training inputs are unchanged. The final workbook was added without recomputing or modifying its data; descriptor-level equivalence to archived inputs has not been re-audited.
